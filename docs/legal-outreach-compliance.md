# Legal Outreach & Regional Compliance Review

## 1. Executive Summary
CareerOps autonomously generates and dispatches professional recruitment and networking outreach on behalf of candidate tenants operating under a legally binding Sprint Contract.
To maintain strict compliance with global anti-spam, data protection, and privacy laws, all outbound communication strictly adheres to **Hard Line H4 (Verified Business Channels Only)** and regional compliance rules across key jurisdictions.

---

## 2. Jurisdiction Analysis & Statutory Requirements

### 2.1 United States (CAN-SPAM Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 7701–7713)
* **Application**: Applies to all commercial electronic mail messages sent to recipients in the US.
* **Requirements Enforced**:
  1. **Accurate Header Information**: Sender headers (`From:`, `Reply-To:`, domain name) must accurately reflect the authenticated candidate.
  2. **Non-Deceptive Subject Lines**: Subject lines must accurately describe the content of the message (enforced by `OutreachEngine` prompt templates).
  3. **Sender Physical Postal Address**: All outreach footers must contain a valid physical postal address of the candidate or CareerOps registered agent (`SenderIdentity.physicalAddress`).
  4. **Clear and Conspicuous Opt-Out**: Every email must include an explicit opt-out mechanism (URL link or direct reply instruction *"reply 'unsubscribe' or 'opt out'"*).
  5. **Prompt Suppression Honoring**: Any opt-out request must be processed and suppressed within 10 business days. CareerOps enforces **immediate real-time suppression** via `SuppressionPropagator`.
  6. **No Harvesting or Probing**: Strictly aligns with Hard Line H4 — no guessed or SMTP-probed addresses permitted.

### 2.2 European Union (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) & ePrivacy Directive)
* **Application**: Electronic mail sent to individuals residing in the European Economic Area.
* **Legal Basis**:
  * Cold B2B recruitment outreach to corporate email addresses (`@company.com`) is conducted under **GDPR Article 6(1)(f) (Legitimate Interests)**.
  * *Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA)*:
    1. *Purpose Test*: Professional inquiry regarding active engineering vacancies or team expansion.
    2. *Necessity Test*: Direct message to hiring decision-makers is the least intrusive means to explore bilateral employment.
    3. *Balancing Test*: Recipient is contacted strictly at their corporate role, about role-relevant topics, with zero personal profiling and immediate right to object.
* **Requirements Enforced**:
  1. Corporate B2B email addresses only (`name@company.com`). Personal email domains (`@gmail.com`, `@yahoo.com`, `@hotmail.com`) are strictly rejected.
  2. Clear disclosure of the data controller and legitimate interest basis in the footer.
  3. Direct notice of GDPR Article 21 **Right to Object (Opt-Out)** honored immediately.
  4. Data minimization: only corporate name, title, and public corporate contact details retained.

### 2.3 United Kingdom (UK GDPR & PECR)
* **Application**: B2B electronic mail under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR Regulation 22/23).
* **Requirements Enforced**:
  1. PECR permits unsolicited B2B emails to corporate subscribers (employees of companies) without prior opt-in consent, provided the sender identifies themselves and provides a valid opt-out mechanism.
  2. Compliance with UK GDPR Article 13/14 transparency requirements.

### 2.4 Canada (CASL, S.C. 2010, c. 23)
* **Application**: Commercial electronic messages (CEM) routed through or sent to computer systems in Canada.
* **Exemption & Rules**:
  1. Relies on **Section 6(5)(b)** B2B business inquiry exemption for inquiries relating to the commercial activities of the recipient organization.
  2. Mandatory sender identification, postal address, and functional unsubscribe mechanism valid for at least 60 days.

### 2.5 India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)
* **Application**: Processing of digital personal data within India.
* **Requirements Enforced**:
  1. Explicit statement of purpose (professional career inquiry).
  2. Direct sender contact info for grievance redressal and opt-out processing.

## 3. Compliance Footer Implementation

Every outbound message generated by `OutreachEngine` appends an immutable compliance footer formatted per region:

```typescript
// OutreachComplianceEngine.generateComplianceFooter()
switch (jurisdiction) {
  case 'US':
    return `\n\n---\nSent by ${addressText}\n${optOutText}`;
  case 'EU':
  case 'UK':
    return `\n\n---\nCommunication sent by ${addressText} based on legitimate professional interest. We process your contact details solely for direct professional correspondence.\n${optOutText}`;
  case 'CA':
    return `\n\n---\nCommunication sent by ${addressText} as a direct business inquiry under CASL s. 6(5)(b).\n${optOutText}`;
  case 'IN':
    return `\n\n---\nSender: ${addressText}. Purpose: Professional career inquiry.\n${optOutText}`;
  default:
    return `\n\n---\nSent by ${addressText}\n${optOutText}`;
}
```

## 4. Verification Checklists Before Outreach Send
Before any outreach email is dispatched:
1. `assertAuthorizedContact(email, source)`: Verification source must be `licensed_provider`, `public_company_page`, or `user_imported_connection`.
2. `assertProvenanceEvidence(email, evidence)`: Validates HTTPS URL, content SHA-256 hash, retrieval timestamp, and provider record ID.
3. `isSuppressed(email, suppressionList)`: Checks local tenant and global suppression databases.
4. `checkQuietHours(recipientTimeZone, now)`: Blocks dispatch between 8:00 PM and 8:00 AM recipient local time.
5. `adaptiveCapManager.canSend(tenantId)`: Evaluates daily volume limits and health metrics.
